# 🛡️⚔️ Understand Types of Attackers

> ### 🌐 **Cybersecurity Fundamentals**
> Attackers are individuals or groups who attempt to gain unauthorized access, steal information, disrupt services, or compromise systems. Their **motivation, skill level, resources, and targets** can vary significantly.

---

## 🧑‍💻 01 — Script Kiddies

> 📝 **Definition:**  
> Script kiddies are individuals who use **pre-existing hacking tools, scripts, or exploits** without necessarily understanding how they work.

### 🔹 Key Characteristics

- 🧠 Limited technical knowledge
- 🛠️ Depend heavily on existing tools
- 🎮 Often motivated by curiosity, fun, or recognition
- ⚠️ May cause accidental damage

### 💡 Example

> A beginner uses a publicly available security tool against a website without understanding the vulnerability being exploited.

---

## 💰 02 — Cyber Criminals

> 📝 **Definition:**  
> Cyber criminals use computers and networks to commit crimes, usually for **financial gain**.

### 🔹 Key Characteristics

- 💵 Primarily motivated by money
- 🎯 Target individuals, businesses, and organizations
- 🔐 May steal credentials or sensitive information
- 🦠 Can use malware, phishing, and ransomware

### 💡 Example

> An attacker tricks a victim into revealing their login credentials and attempts to use the account for financial fraud.

---

## 🏢 03 — Insider Threats

> 📝 **Definition:**  
> An insider threat comes from someone who already has **legitimate access** to an organization's systems or information.

### 🔹 Types of Insider Threats

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| 🔴 **Malicious Insider** | Intentionally misuses their access |
| 🟡 **Negligent Insider** | Accidentally causes a security problem |
| 🟠 **Compromised Insider** | Their account or credentials are taken over |

### 💡 Example

> An employee accidentally sends confidential company information to an unauthorized person.

---

## ✊ 04 — Hacktivists

> 📝 **Definition:**  
> Hacktivists use cyber activities to promote **political, social, or ideological causes**.

### 🔹 Key Characteristics

- ✊ Motivated by beliefs or causes
- 🎯 Target organizations they oppose
- 🌐 May attempt website disruption or defacement
- 👥 Can operate as groups or movements

### 💡 Example

> A group targets an organization's online services because it disagrees with the organization's policies.

---

## 🌐 05 — Nation-State Actors

> 📝 **Definition:**  
> Nation-state actors are highly capable attackers **associated with or supported by governments**.

### 🔹 Key Characteristics

- 💻 Advanced technical capabilities
- 💰 Significant financial resources
- 🎯 May target governments and critical infrastructure
- 🕵️ Can conduct long-term cyber espionage
- 🌍 May pursue strategic or national interests

### 💡 Example

> A government-backed group conducts a long-term campaign to obtain sensitive information from another country's government systems.

---

| 🛡️ Attacker | 🎯 Main Motivation | 💪 Typical Resources |
|---|---|---|
| 🧑‍💻 **Script Kiddies** | Curiosity / Fun | ⭐ Low |

| 🏢 **Insider Threats** | Varies | ⭐⭐ Varies |

| 🌐 **Nation-State Actors** | Espionage / Strategic Goals | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very High |

---

## 🔐🎯 Key Takeaway

> ### **Different attackers → Different motivations → Different capabilities**
>
> Understanding **who the attacker is** helps cybersecurity professionals identify potential **threats, targets, attack methods, risks, and appropriate defenses**.

# 🔎 Understand OSINT Fundamentals

> **OSINT (Open-Source Intelligence)** is the process of collecting, analyzing, and verifying information from publicly available sources.

---

## 🌐 a. Open-Source Intelligence

**OSINT** means gathering intelligence from information that is publicly accessible.

**Examples:**

* 🌍 Websites
* 📰 News articles
* 📱 Social media
* 📚 Public databases
* 🗂️ Online reports

---

## 🔍 b. Public Information Gathering

Public information gathering involves collecting useful information from **legal and publicly available sources**.

### Key Points

* 🔎 Find relevant information
* 📝 Record important findings
* ✅ Verify information
* 🔐 Respect privacy and applicable laws

---

## 🧠 c. Search Techniques

Search techniques are methods used to **find relevant, accurate, and useful information efficiently**.

### Common Techniques

| Technique           | Purpose                          |
| ------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 🔑 Keywords         | Find relevant information        |
| `"Exact phrase"`    | Search for an exact phrase       |
| `site:`             | Search within a specific website |
| `filetype:`         | Find specific file types         |
| 🔄 Multiple sources | Compare information              |

> 💡 **Tip:** Always verify important findings using multiple reliable sources.

---

## 🛡️ d. Threat Research

OSINT can be used in cybersecurity to research and understand potential **threats and risks**.

### Areas of Research

* 🦠 Malware
* 🎣 Phishing campaigns
* ⚠️ Vulnerabilities
* 👥 Threat actors
* 🌐 Attack trends
* 📰 Security incidents

The goal is to understand threats and help organizations **identify and reduce security risks**.

---

## 🕵️ e. Investigation Workflows

A structured workflow helps keep an OSINT investigation organized.

### 🔄 Basic Workflow

**Define → Collect → Verify → Analyze → Document → Report**

### 1️⃣ Define

Clearly identify what you are trying to find.

### 2️⃣ Collect

Gather information from relevant public sources.

### 3️⃣ Verify

Check whether the information is accurate and trustworthy.

### 4️⃣ Analyze

Connect and interpret the collected information.

### 5️⃣ Document

Record important evidence and sources.

### 6️⃣ Report

Present the findings clearly and objectively.

---

## 🎯 Key Takeaway

> **OSINT = Collect → Verify → Analyze → Report**

OSINT is an important cybersecurity skill that helps security professionals **research threats, investigate incidents, and understand publicly available information** while following ethical and legal boundaries.

# 🛡️ Understand Common Attack Techniques

> 📚 **Cybersecurity Learning Notes**

---

## 🎣 a. Phishing

**Phishing** is a cyberattack where an attacker pretends to be a trusted person or organization to trick a victim into providing sensitive information.



---

## 📊 Main Types

| 🎯 **Type**           | 📝 **Simple Explanation**                                                                       | 👤 **Target**                    | 💡 **Example**                                                                                     |
| :-------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 📧 **Email Phishing** | A fake email that pretends to come from a trusted person or organization to trick the user.     | 👥 Many people                   | 🏦 A fake bank email asking you to verify your account.                                            |
| 🎯 **Spear Phishing** | A targeted phishing attack designed specifically for one person using personalized information. | 👤 A specific person or employee | 🏢 An attacker sends an employee a fake email using their name and company details.                |
| 🐋 **Whaling**        | A specialized form of spear phishing that targets important people in an organization.          | 👔 CEOs, managers, executives    | 💼 A fake message appears to come from a CEO asking an employee to share confidential information. |

---

## 🧠 Easy Way to Remember

```text
📧 Email Phishing
       ↓
Targets MANY people

🎯 Spear Phishing
       ↓
Targets ONE specific person

🐋 Whaling
       ↓
Targets HIGH-PROFILE people
```

---

## 🔐 Key Difference

| Type                  | 🎯 Targeting Level |
| :-------------------- | :----------------: |
| 📧 **Email Phishing** |     🟢 General     |
| 🎯 **Spear Phishing** |     🟠 Specific    |
| 🐋 **Whaling**        |   🔴 High-Profile  |

---

> 💡 **Remember:**
> **Phishing → Spear Phishing → Whaling**
> General target → Specific target → High-profile target


### 🔹 Examples

* Fake bank emails
* Fake login pages
* Suspicious links
* Malicious email attachments

### 🎯 Goal

To steal:

* 🔑 Passwords
* 👤 Personal information
* 💳 Financial information
* 📧 Account credentials

---

## 🧠 b. Social Engineering

**Social engineering** is the use of psychological manipulation to trick people into revealing information or performing an action.

Instead of directly attacking a computer, the attacker targets **human behavior**.

### 🔹 Examples

* Pretending to be technical support
* Creating a false sense of urgency
* Impersonating an employee or trusted person
* Asking for confidential information

### 🎯 Goal

To manipulate victims into:

* Sharing sensitive information
* Opening suspicious files or links
* Giving unauthorized access

---

## 🔑 c. Credential Attacks

**Credential attacks** are attacks where an attacker tries to **steal, guess, or misuse someone's login information,** such as a username and password.

### 🔹 Common Types

| Attack                     | Description                                           |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 🔨 **Brute Force**         | Trying many possible passwords                        |
| 🎯 **Password Spraying**   | Trying a common password against many accounts        |
| ♻️ **Credential Stuffing** | Using previously leaked credentials on other services |
| ⌨️ **Keylogging**          | Recording keystrokes using malicious software         |

### 🎯 Goal

To gain **unauthorized access** to accounts or systems.

---

## 🦠 d. Malware Delivery

**Malware delivery** is the process of getting malicious software onto a victim's device.

### 🔹 Common Delivery Methods

* 📧 Malicious email attachments
* 🔗 Malicious links
* 💻 Fake or compromised software
* 🌐 Compromised websites
* 💾 Infected removable media

### 🎯 Goal

To install malware such as:

* 🦠 Viruses
* 🐴 Trojans
* 🕵️ Spyware
* 🔒 Ransomware

> 🛡️ **Safety Note:** Malware should only be analyzed in an isolated and authorized cybersecurity lab.

---

## 🔎 e. Reconnaissance Activities

**Reconnaissance** is the information-gathering stage of an attack. Attackers collect information about a target before attempting further actions.

### 🔹 Information That May Be Collected

* 🌐 Domain names
* 📍 IP addresses
* 💻 Technologies and software used
* 👥 Publicly available employee information
* 🔓 Publicly exposed services
* 📱 Public information from websites and social media

### 🔹 Types of Reconnaissance

**1. Passive Reconnaissance**

Collecting information without directly interacting with the target's systems.

**Example:**
Researching publicly available information on websites.

**2. Active Reconnaissance**

Interacting directly with the target's systems to gather information.

**Example:**
Checking publicly exposed services on an authorized test system.

### 🎯 Goal

To understand the target environment and identify potential weaknesses.

## 💡 Key Takeaway

Understanding common attack techniques helps cybersecurity professionals **recognize threats, reduce risks, and build better defenses**.

> 🔐 **Learn attacks to understand how to defend against them.**
